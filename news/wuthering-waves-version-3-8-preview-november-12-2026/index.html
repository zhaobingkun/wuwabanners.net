Wuthering Waves Version 3.8 Arrives November 12

Kuro confirmed that Version 3.8, Lilies in a Dying Dream, launches on November 12, 2026.

Banner impact: The official preview confirms the Version 3.8 release date and Reverb Convene availability, but does not provide a complete phase lineup, rerun order, or featured-weapon schedule for the confirmed banner dataset.

Published: November 6, 2026. Event time: Version 3.8 launches November 12, 2026 at UTC+8.

What happened

Kuro Games published the official Wuthering Waves Version Preview for Version 3.8, Lilies in a Dying Dream, on November 6, 2026.

The notice confirms that Version 3.8 is scheduled to release on November 12, 2026 at UTC+8. It also says that Reverb Resonator and Reverb Weapon Convenes will be available after the update.

For banner tracking, this is an official version-preview notice only. It does not publish a complete phase lineup, rerun order, or featured-weapon schedule, so WuWa Banners leaves the confirmed banner CSV unchanged until those facts are verified by an official Convene notice.
