Wuthering Waves Announces Tiered Client Resource Downloads

Kuro announced tiered resource packages for PC and mobile without changing confirmed banner facts.

Banner impact: The notice describes a limited test of UHD, HD, and SD resource packages after Version 3.7; it adds no phase timing, banner order, rerun, or featured weapon facts, so confirmed banner data remains unchanged.

Published: September 24, 2026. Event time: After the Version 3.7 update.

What happened

Kuro Games published an official notice on September 24, 2026 about Tiered Client Resource Downloads, a limited test that lets players choose resource packages based on device storage and specifications.

The notice describes UHD, HD, and SD package tiers for PC and HD/SD tiers for iOS and Android. The test is scheduled to become available after the Version 3.7 update, with additional package and pre-download details for different platforms.

For banner tracking, this is a client-feature announcement rather than a Convene schedule notice. It does not confirm phase dates, banner order, reruns, or featured weapons, so WuWa Banners leaves the confirmed banner dataset unchanged.
