Wuthering Waves Version 3.8 Maintenance Confirms Lily and Lullaby

Kuro published the Version 3.8 maintenance notice and confirmed Lily and Lullaby for the update.

Banner impact: The official notice confirms Lily and Lullaby as Version 3.8 featured Convene additions, but it does not publish a complete phase schedule or end date, so the confirmed banner CSV remains unchanged.

Published: November 7, 2026. Event time: Version 3.8 maintenance begins November 12, 2026 at 04:00 UTC+8.

What happened

Kuro Games published the official Wuthering Waves Version 3.8 Update Maintenance Notice on November 7, 2026.

The notice schedules Version 3.8 maintenance for November 12, 2026 from 04:00 to 11:00 UTC+8 and confirms the new 5-star Resonator Lily and 5-star pistol Lullaby for the update. It also announces pre-download from November 10 at 10:00 UTC+8.

For banner tracking, this is an official update notice with confirmed Version 3.8 character and weapon names, but it does not provide a complete Phase I or Phase II schedule, rerun order, or banner end date. WuWa Banners therefore records the news while leaving the confirmed banner CSV unchanged until the official Convene timing is published.
