Wuthering Waves Version 3.7 Phase I Convene Details

Kuro confirmed the Version 3.7 Phase I character and weapon Convene lineups and end time.

Banner impact: Phase I runs after the Version 3.7 update through October 22 at 09:59 server time, featuring Hsin and Chisa plus Kumokiri and Moongazer's Sigil; the confirmed banner CSV now records these facts.

Published: September 29, 2026. Event time: September 30, 2026 after the Version 3.7 update through October 22, 2026 at 09:59 server time.

What happened

Kuro Games published the official Version 3.7 Phase I Resonator and weapon Convene notice on September 29, 2026.

The notice confirms that the Phase I activities begin after the Version 3.7 update and run through October 22, 2026 at 09:59 server time. The featured Resonators are Hsin and Chisa, while the featured weapons are Kumokiri and Moongazer's Sigil.

WuWa Banners has added these directly verifiable Phase I dates and lineups to the confirmed banner dataset. Phase II remains separate until its own official timing and lineup are directly published.
