Wuthering Waves Version 3.6 Preview Broadcast Reveals New Update Content

Kuro's Version 3.6 preview broadcast introduced Qingxiao and Jingran, confirmed rerun plans, and detailed the next update's new content.

Banner impact: The broadcast is a real official update and confirms the Version 3.6 banner direction, but this news item does not replace the banner data until the official event calendar provides the exact phase dates and weapon listings in a directly verifiable notice.

What happened

Kuro Games has completed the Wuthering Waves Version 3.6 Preview Special Broadcast, titled Lamplight in Mirage, Sword's Resolve in Heart. The official update page covers the new story content, Ren Realm areas, Sword Flight, events, and platform optimizations.

For banner tracking, the broadcast introduced the new 5-star Resonators Qingxiao and Jingran and showed the planned rerun direction for Denia, Hiyuki, and Mornye. Their associated signature weapons were also presented during the preview.

WuWa Banners will keep the existing Version 3.5 Phase 2 data live until the official Version 3.6 event calendar confirms exact phase start and end times and the complete weapon lineups in a directly verifiable source.
