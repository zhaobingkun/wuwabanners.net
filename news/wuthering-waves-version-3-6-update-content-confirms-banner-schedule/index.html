Wuthering Waves Version 3.6 Banner Schedule Confirmed

Kuro's official Version 3.6 update content confirms both banner phases, dates, characters, and weapons.

Banner impact: Version 3.6 Phase 1 runs August 20 to September 10, followed by Phase 2 from September 10 to September 29; the confirmed banner dataset is updated accordingly.

Published: August 18, 2026. Event time: Version 3.6: August 20, 2026 at 11:00 UTC+8.

What happened

Kuro Games published the official Wuthering Waves Version 3.6 update content on August 18, 2026. The notice confirms the version schedule and the featured Convene lineup for both phases.

Phase 1 runs from August 20 at 11:00 through September 10 at 09:59 server time with Qingxiao and Denia, plus Glint of Clouds and Forged Dwarf Star. Phase 2 begins September 10 at 10:00 and runs through September 29 at 11:59 with Jingran, Hiyuki, and Mornye, plus Thousandfold Deliverance, Frostburn, and Starfield Calibrator.

WuWa Banners has updated its confirmed banner history and current schedule from this official notice. Times are shown in server time because the game's Convene schedule uses that clock.
