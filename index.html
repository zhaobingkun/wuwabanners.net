What counts as a confirmed Wuthering Waves banner update?

A banner update should come from an official notice, in-game Convene information, or another high-confidence source. Version-number mentions, community guesses, and generic feed matches should stay as watchlist signals until they change a real banner lineup, date, source URL, or phase page.

That distinction matters because Wuthering Waves banner searches often mix confirmed banners, rerun hopes, leaks, and old phase references. This homepage should help users move from a broad banner query to the right verified page without overstating uncertain information. When a source does not confirm characters, weapons, dates, or a usable banner window, the site should keep the signal in watch mode and point users back to current banner, next banner, schedule, history, rerun, or pity pages.
