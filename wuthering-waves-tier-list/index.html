How this Wuthering Waves tier list is ranked

Role value

A top DPS must justify field time and team cost. A support can rank just as high by improving several teams, compressing sustain, or adding reliable off-field value.

Team flexibility

Characters that work across more than one strong shell receive more account-value credit than characters locked to a narrow partner or mechanic.

Practical investment

The list assumes a realistic baseline rather than maximum sequences. Signature weapons, premium partners, and exceptional execution can change individual results.

Current endgame fit

Tower of Adversity favors focused boss teams, while Whimpering Wastes can reward fast rotations and wave control. One combined tier cannot describe every floor perfectly.

How to use the tier list before pulling

  • Check whether the character fills a missing DPS, hybrid, sustain, or support role.
  • Compare the required partners with characters already built on your account.
  • Open the current banner and pity pages before turning a high tier into an automatic pull.
  • Prefer a complete team over collecting several top DPS characters that compete for the same supports.

Current banner context

Hiyuki and Mornye sit in the current top group. Jingran is deliberately unranked while Phase 2 testing settles. A live banner can still be a skip if its role does not improve your account.

Wuthering Waves tier list FAQ

Who are the best characters in Wuthering Waves Version 3.6?

The top general-value group includes Qingxiao, Aemeath, Hiyuki, Sigrika, Yangyang: Xuanling, Suisui, Cartethyia, Phrolova, Iuno, Galbrena, Ciaccona, Lupa, Lynae, Mornye, Shorekeeper, and Lucilla. Pick for the role your account lacks.

Is Jingran ranked yet?

Not yet. Jingran entered the live phase on September 10, 2026, so this page keeps the character on a watchlist until more stable endgame testing is available.

Does a lower tier mean a character is unusable?

No. Weapons, Echoes, sequences, teammates, encounter rules, and player execution can move results substantially. The tiers describe likely resource return, not whether a character can clear content.

How often will this tier list change?

Review it after a new version, a meaningful endgame rules change, or enough testing of a newly released Resonator. Small popularity shifts alone are not enough to rewrite the list.
